# data/breakout.json

R82 — The Breakout. A rival lab that keeps losing to you starts losing other things: specimens. Each escapee is a REAL rival chimera, built by campaign/rivals.js's own generator from that lab's parts at that lab's grades, so a loose one is indistinguishable from one still on the ladder — because it was on the ladder. Unlike a counter-offensive (regions.json contestation) an escapee has NO deadline: it accumulates on a standing board and waits, because the point of the system is more fights with rival anatomy, and a window that closes while the player is asleep produces fewer of them, not more. It is gated on `startsAfterDefeats` so nothing gets loose until you have actually rattled somebody. Beating one closes the entry either way; the Containment Cannon decides whether it goes home in a bay or is collected by its old lab, and a bagged one reaches the roster the same way every captive does — the Reorientation Wing (campaign/rehab.js). maxLoose caps the board so a player who ignores it does not return to a wall of them.

## release

R129 — THE LAST LAB FALLS OPEN. Beating the fifth rival was the one rung on the ladder with no consequence. Measured first: five 180-day walks already spawn ~190 escapees and the board is EMPTY on every seed, so `maxLoose` above never binds and the drip was never the problem. Two ceilings were — the five lab palettes union to 21 of the 41 species, so half the bestiary can never be loose; and a bagged specimen is not worth a stall, because a Wing graduate carries its old lab's grades (1,035 bagged in a walk, ONE kept). So the release is a PHASE: it fires once, it opens the county to anatomy no lab owns, and what gets out carries a mutation trait the player cannot roll. Wire copy lives in news.json under `labs_open`, as all of it does; `line`, `blurb` and `intel` are the Labs board's own three sentences.
