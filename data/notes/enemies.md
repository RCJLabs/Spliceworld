# data/enemies.json

Gen 1 human enemy roster (ROADMAP §3.6) + encounters. Units: stats, unit tags (Organic/Vehicle plus battle tags), moves (same schema as part moves), koLine (zero death language — soldiers parachute away, vehicles retire loudly), shapes (procedural SVG in unit-local space, viewBox -100 -110 200 220). transformInto = boss second stage. Encounters: ordered wave of unit ids + reward funds. Adding a unit or encounter must never require engine edits. Art (M7.5): organics carry googly eyes under the brim; vehicles get windshield eyes with angry brows — machines are characters too. class: the unit's elemental class for the RPS chart in data/classes.json. tier + tierScale: the difficulty curve, and aiSkillByTier is how well the opposition PLAYS at each rung (R67: the engine held six rungs against nine of scaling, so tiers 6-8 were all piloted at tier 5). Unit stats (hp/power/armor/stamina) are multiplied by tierScale[tier] at battle time, so one authored roster covers the whole campaign and a later encounter is genuinely harder without duplicating units. tierScale[0] is unused; tier 1 is the tutorial patrol and tier 8 is the Compliance Spire. Rival chimeras carry their own powerScale instead (see rivals.json) and are never tier-scaled. A5: every part listed in a unit's `salvage` has to come off a unit that is actually FIELDED — on a node, in an off-node encounter, or by a director counter-rule. foghorn_array was reachable only from leviathan_dredge, which appears in no encounter at all, so the armour-piercing answer to the Foundry was unobtainable while the balance harness went on benching the Foundry with it.
