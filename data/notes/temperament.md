# data/temperament.json

Temperament (ROADMAP §3.5): two axes, Skittish–Brave (`nerve`) and Gentle–Fierce (`temper`), each -100..+100. Seeded at SETTLING from the chimera's dominant donor species — the one that contributed the most parts — because §3.5 says temperament comes from the anatomy, and then DRIFTED by how you raise it: training makes a creature braver and gentler, winning makes it fiercer, being knocked out makes it warier. Biases are keyed by species ROLE rather than by species, so a designer can say 'Walls are calm and steady' once instead of thirty-two times; `bySpecies` overrides where a particular animal has a character its role does not capture. Perks are passive stat effects only — §3.5's 'never removes player control' rules out anything that takes a turn away, which is what obedience already does and what this must not duplicate.
