# data/resequencer.json

The Resequencer (R31). A DNA vial has existed since M2 and was read by NOTHING — extraction produced one every time and the Gene Vault listed them, forever, as a growing pile of inventory that did nothing. This is what spends them. A vial is the whole donor's essence, so spending one grows that donor back: same species, same star potential, same genotype — which matters because extraction was previously the one irreversible act in the game. Graduate your best recessive carrier and those genes were gone. Now they are banked. THE TRADE, and it is deliberately a real one: the run takes real-world hours, the vial is consumed either way, and one in four collapses. `successBase` is the flat odds; quality does not buy safety, it buys UPSIDE — `mutationBase + stars * mutationPerStar` is the chance the returning animal comes back with a gene neither it nor its donor had, so banking a five-star vial is worth more than banking four ordinary ones. The Incubator track governs both halves (it is an incubation): `hourScale` shortens the run and `mutationBonus` multiplies the mutation odds, exactly as it does for breeding, so the track gains a second reason to exist without a single new facility knob. Outcome is SEALED at launch from a seeded stream, like the vat and the jobs board: reloading the app cannot reroll a failure into a success.
