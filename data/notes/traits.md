# data/traits.json

Heritable trait genes (M6, ROADMAP §3.2). Animals carry 0-2 alleles per trait (genotype); dominant traits express with 1+, recessive need 2. Each parent passes one allele with probability (alleles/2). R24: `wildChance` puts a gene into ordinary mail-order stock so it can be FOUND and bred up -- which is what makes the Mendel machinery worth having; `mutationOnly` traits stay out of the pool and arrive only as a conception mutation. Mechanical teeth: when a donor expressing the trait is extracted, part tokens in the listed slots are stamped with it; `statBonus` is applied by the physiology engine and `moveKeywords` is merged into those parts' moves, under the part's own keywords so a gene never overwrites what a part already did. Every trait pays for what it gives. Adding traits must never require engine edits.
