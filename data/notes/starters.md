# data/starters.json

The founding choice (ROADMAP §9.7, R119). Measured on a fresh save, the starter herd was a literal ['goat','goat','bear'] with the goats newborn, so the only animal graduatable on day one was the bear — and a graduation yields six parts from ONE species. The Theater therefore opened with exactly one creature anybody could build. A lab is three things: a GROWN DONOR (extractable immediately, the day-one door A4 opened), a BREEDABLE JUVENILE PAIR, and a CRATE of two parts from a third species. THE CRATE HOLDS NO HEAD, deliberately: a head is mandatory to splice, so a crate with one lets a new player build a two-part creature out of the crate alone before meeting the extractor. Without one, the sequence A4 fixed holds — graduate, THEN splice. It is the forelimbs and hindlimbs because those most change an animal and, measured, decide whether it can fight. THE CHOICE CHANGES WHICH CREATURE, NEVER HOW MUCH: three animals, one grown, all Standard, under every lab. Against the second node the five read 0% with one chimera and 96/96/92/100/100% with three, so A1's wall holds and R106's promise is true whichever you pick. That balance was searched, not guessed. Adding a sixth lab is this file and nothing else.
