# data/chaos.json

Chaos-breeding (ROADMAP §9 backlog). Ranch breeding pairs two ANIMALS of one species and produces a predictable hybrid of their stats. This is the other thing: two finished CHIMERAS go into a vat and what comes out is a genome neither of them was, assembled by nobody. THE ECONOMY IS THE DESIGN PROBLEM. A chimera costs vault tokens permanently, and chimeras carry no upkeep, so an offspring that costs only money and time would be a duplication glitch — you would breed an army for free. The cost is therefore paid in GRADES, which is the game's real power currency: both parents permanently drop one grade on every token ('they gave a lot of themselves'). That makes a line get weaker each generation unless you keep injecting freshly-raised high-grade stock, which is exactly the ranch loop this game is about. Standard-grade parents cannot degrade further, so the fee scales with parent quality to stop cheap junk being free. WHAT MAKES IT WORTH DOING: the vat does not read your permits. It can hand you a socket layout your Surgery Theater is not licensed to build, a frame you do not own, and — via chaosChance — a part from neither parent, drawn from the Splice-Dex so it is always something you have SEEN and never a gift from the end of the game.
