# data/parts-shapes.json

R81: the drawing half of data/parts.json, split out because it was 69% of the file and is read by exactly one module (render/renderer.js). Keyed by part id. NOT fetched before the first paint — data/loader.js pulls it in after the shell is on screen and merges it onto content.parts[id].shapes, so every reader is unchanged. Generated by tools/gen-parts.js alongside data/parts.json; the two are written together and smoke asserts every part has geometry and every geometry entry has a part.
