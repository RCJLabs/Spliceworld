# data/parts-shapes.json

R81: the drawing half of data/parts.json, split out because it was 69% of the file and is read by exactly one module (render/renderer.js). Keyed by part id. NOT fetched before the first paint — data/loader.js pulls it in after the shell is on screen and merges it onto content.parts[id].shapes, so every reader is unchanged. Generated by tools/gen-parts.js alongside data/parts.json; the two are written together and smoke asserts every part has geometry and every geometry entry has a part.

## shapes

**Parts that move (R206).** A shape may carry `anim`, a class name. The renderer writes it as the shape's
`class` and nothing else; `style.css` decides what the class does, and a
`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` block turns it off. So data says WHICH layer
moves and the stylesheet says HOW, and a new animated part is a JSON field and
a CSS rule, never an engine edit. The shape's own `opacity` is its still frame,
which is all a player who asked for reduced motion sees, and all an exported
specimen card (a standalone SVG with no stylesheet) ever shows.

The first part to use it is the cuttlefish's mantle: three layers of patches,
`shift-a`, `shift-b` and `shift-c`, fade on one six-second clock started two
seconds apart, so the coat moves through its palette. `tools/smoke.js` checks
the chain from the data end: every `anim` a part names has an animation in the
stylesheet and an off-switch under reduced motion, and the renderer writes it.
`tools/a11y.js` checks the same off-switch from the stylesheet end.


## anchors

R214 — where each generated head wears things, keyed by part id: `crown` (the
top of the skull and its half-width), `eye` (the near eye and its radius) and
`neck` (under the jaw), each in the head's own space. They come from
`HEAD_ANCHORS` in tools/shapes.js, declared beside the template that draws
the head, with the same parameters and defaults, so a new species' head gets
anchors from its template and a template change moves them with it. Salvage
heads are hand-made and have none; the wardrobe hangs everything off their
near eye. Read only by render/cosmetics.js (data/notes/cosmetics.md).
