# data/tiers.json

R125 - the chimera tier scale (F..S). Every number here was measured, not chosen: see ROADMAP 9.11 and tools/tierfit.js. The model predicts a build's SOLO WIN RATE over the whole shipped encounter table, and a letter is a band of that rate. Adding a tier, moving a cut or reweighting a term is a data edit; the engine reads this file and knows nothing about F or S.
