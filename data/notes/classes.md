# data/classes.json

Elemental classes (Wave 1). A chimera's class is DERIVED from its parts, never assigned: each part may carry classAffinity ('air' | 'ground' | 'water'), granted by anatomy — wings, hindwings, membranes and talons give air, gills/fins/flippers/webbed feet and a swimming bell give water, paws/hooves/walking legs/horned skulls give ground. A head votes only where the anatomy is class-defining (gills, a bell, a beak, horns), which is why most heads do not vote at all. Audit A3 rebalanced this: the pool was 36 Ground parts to 25 Water and 9 Air, with Air holding nothing at all in four of the six sockets, so an Air specialist could not be built out of Air anatomy — it had to borrow legs and then lose the vote it borrowed them for. The class with the most parts wins; a tie leaves the creature Unclassed (neutral both directions), so committing to one anatomy buys the matchup edge while hybrids trade it for safety. chart: attacker class vs defender class -> damage multiplier. Enemies declare their class directly in enemies.json.
