# data/keywords.json

The ability keyword system (ROADMAP §4.2): every move = power/cost/acc + 1-2 keywords + numbers, no bespoke scripts. battle/engine.js resolves keyword semantics; this file is the design reference (names/descriptions) plus the data-driven tag chart. Move keyword params: recoil (fraction of damage dealt, self), venom (stacks added), stun/sleep (proc chance), trap/guard/priority/charge/ignoreArmor/ignoreGuard/knockback (true), accUp/accDown/powerUp/powerDown/evasionUp (stat stages, ±15%/stage), staminaRestore (flat), heal (fraction of max HP). tagChart rules: attacker move tag vs defender tag -> mult (0 = misses entirely) or rule 'ignoreArmor'. R149: a defender tag needs BOTH signs or it is not a decision — `Camo` shipped with only `Sonic x1.5` against it, which prices the six chameleon parts below zero; it now also carries `Aimed x0`. `Camo` is the one defender tag that must be EARNED rather than declared: splice/physiology.js strips it from any creature with armour above zero, and armour comes only from hides, so the price of hiding is the hide bay. R20: every keyword here is implemented in battle/engine.js AND carried by at least one move — smoke asserts both, so "Reserved" entries can never accumulate again. Dropped as redundant: reflect (duplicate of thorns); camouflage (duplicate of evasionUp); aoe (the fight is one active per side over a queue; nothing to splash); suppression (already expressible as accDown + multiHit). `effect` (R30) is what a move carrying this keyword does to THIS creature, written in second person with the move's own magnitude substituted: {n} for a count, {pct} for a fraction rendered as a percentage. `desc` still describes the keyword in the abstract for the Dex. The split exists because 41% of the game's 271 moves are power-0 utility moves, and the battle screen used to render every one of them as the word 'util' and nothing else.
