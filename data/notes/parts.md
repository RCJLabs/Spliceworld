# data/parts.json

Part tokens. M4: move = the part's battle move (null = passive part; its stats still count) with power/cost(stamina)/acc/tags/keywords per keywords.json. Every part = stat-free M0 stub (name, ability flavor) + a 'shapes' drawing spec interpreted by render/renderer.js. Shape types: path, ellipse, circle, rect, polygon, line. Color tokens @primary/@secondary/@accent resolve to the part's species palette; @outline/@white resolve globally. Filled shapes default to a thick @outline stroke unless stroke is set. Local space conventions live in data/notes/frames.md. Limb parts are drawn once per near/far socket by the engine. Wave 1: parts are generated by tools/gen-parts.js from the species roster and the archetype shape library (tools/shapes.js) — a dev tool, not a build step; the game loads this JSON as-is. classAffinity is the anatomy that votes for a chimera's elemental class (see data/classes.json). Hand-authored content lives in the generator too — R20's keyword moves, R23's hide and organ actives, and R127's HAND_TUNED (forty parts whose abilities, moves and one passive were written straight into this file, and which running the generator used to revert) — because a generator that reverts four phases of tuning the next time somebody runs it is a trap. R32: phys.mass, phys.draw and phys.lift are no longer slot constants — every one of the 41 species used to total exactly 58 mass — but the species' `bulk` (what the animal weighs) times a DENSITY keyed on the part's shape family (what it is made of). A wing is a hollow spar under a membrane; a plate hide is armour; they never should have massed the same. Art style: bold flat vector — thick @outline strokes on masses, thinner on detail; two googly eyes with catchlights on heads; @white low-opacity sheen for form; @secondary for muzzles/bellies, @accent for horns/beaks/claws.
