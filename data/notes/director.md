# data/director.json

AI Director (ROADMAP §3.7). The world studies you: it reads the anatomy you actually field, the parts you keep splicing, and — most sharply — every chimera you let the enemy dissect, then biases the next encounter toward whatever answers you. Each rule declares what it READS (player tags and/or an elemental class) and which roster units answer it; the director swaps ONE wave slot, never the opening beat and never the tutorial tier, so an encounter stays recognisably itself. Everything here is data: adding a counter is a JSON edit, and a rule whose units do not exist is simply skipped. intel is what the briefing tells you — the director must always be legible, never a silent difficulty knob (Law 4). The director's REACH starts at the top tier and works down as you take territory and lose creatures, so a legible stable is pressured at the hard end of the ladder first rather than everywhere at once. It never cuts an encounter's opening beat, its final wave, or a unit that transforms — a boss fight must still contain its boss.
