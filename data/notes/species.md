# data/species.json

Base fauna (34 species; 40 animals with variants, ROADMAP §4.1 and audit §9.2 A3). Palettes drive part rendering via @primary/@secondary/@accent tokens. tags feed the battle chart; class is the species' natural elemental class (a chimera's actual class is derived from its parts' classAffinity — see data/classes.json); archetype selects the shape family used by tools/gen-parts.js. Ranch fields: frame = torso for a purebred; diet/feedCost = one feed action; upkeepPerDay = housing drain; mailOrderPrice = catalog price (null = not sold, acquired by conquering region nodes that unlock its fauna); growthHours = age-stage thresholds; thermal = [minC,maxC] comfort band, intersected across a chimera's species; setBonus = purebred bonus at 4+ parts; incubationMinutes = egg timer. VARIANTS (post-v0.1, ROADMAP §3.2): a species with `variantOf` is the same stock, mutated — it inherits its base's anatomy and frame, and carries its own palette, tags, thermal band, set bonus and `statMult`. Never strictly better: every variant trades something, or the base species becomes dead content the moment you breed one. `affinity` overrides a slot's classAffinity (a Glider Skunk's forelimbs vote Air), and `shapeTweak` nudges the generator's shape params so a variant is not merely a recolour. mailOrderPrice is null on all of them — variants are bred, never bought. `moveTag` appends a tag to a variant's damaging MOVES, which is where the tag chart actually reads — a variant whose identity only shows up on the defending side is a dead mechanic. R32: `bulk` is what the ANIMAL weighs, and it is the multiplier tools/gen-parts.js applies to every part's mass and lift. Before it, mass was a constant — all 41 species' anatomy totalled exactly 58, so a rhino's head massed what a moth's head massed. That was harmless while mass only bought turn order; A9 made mass gate flight (lift >= mass) and cost a point of speed per 50, which meant the game's widest build decision was priced in a currency carrying no information about the creature. Lift follows bulk^(2/3) — wing AREA, not wing mass, which is why a moth gets airborne on nothing and a swan needs a runway. Metabolic draw is deliberately NOT scaled: it was, briefly, and it cost the harness's `gills` archetype four stamina a turn and dropped kestrel/cloudbase from 54% to 21%. Draw is the stamina economy and the upkeep bill; mass and lift are the physics. A variant inherits its base's bulk unless the mutation IS a weight change: an Iron Tortoise is heavier than the tortoise, a Glider Skunk lighter than the skunk, because a patagium is only worth having on something light. `frame` was reassessed at the same time: the cobras have no limbs and rode a quadruped chassis, so they now ride the A-class Kite, and six species whose frame flattered their size (armadillo, chameleon, pangolin, porcupine, goose, heron) dropped to the Scamper. R34: `setBonus.effect` is what makes the purebred bonus real. Every species declared a name and a sentence, and NOTHING read either — physiology computed `purebredSpecies`, handed it to the combatant as `physiology.purebred`, and the battle engine never looked. Measured on matched builds first (same frame, same grade, the same six sockets, bond 100 on both), a purebred build TRAILED a mixed one by 0.1 to 1.8pp across six frame x grade cells: the set was a cost with no payment, because purebred's one wired benefit — 20 less instability — buys nothing once bond cancels the disobedience term, which it does in full at bond 100. So the bonus is not a buff that threatens mixing; it is the missing payment. Three dials, all of them things the engine already reads every turn, so a 42nd species is a data edit and never an engine one: `stats` multiplies the combatant's base numbers, `perks` adds to the temperament perk block, and `keywords` multiplies the NUMERIC value of that keyword wherever it appears on this creature's moves (a boolean keyword has no dial and is left alone). Every `desc` states exactly what its effect does, because the previous ones did not: 14 of the 41 promised a percentage on a keyword that is a boolean — '+20% trap duration' on a trap that has no duration — and those were rewritten rather than faked. Measured after: purebred goes from -0.9pp to +1.3pp, ahead in all six cells, which is a reason to chase a set and not a reason to stop mixing.
